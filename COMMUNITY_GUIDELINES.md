# RAUT Community Guidelines

**Building a Safe, Respectful Community for Everyone**

---

## Our Commitment

RAUT is committed to creating a safe, inclusive transportation platform for all users in Tanzania. These Community Guidelines outline expected behavior and help us maintain a positive environment for riders and drivers.

**Everyone using RAUT must:**
- Treat others with respect and dignity
- Follow all local laws and regulations
- Prioritize safety above all else
- Be honest and accountable
- Help create a welcoming community

Violations of these guidelines may result in warnings, temporary suspension, or permanent account removal.

---

## 1. Safety First

### Physical Safety

**✅ DO:**
- Wear seatbelts at all times
- Verify driver/rider identity before entering vehicle
- Follow traffic laws
- Report unsafe driving immediately
- Trust your instincts - decline ride if uncomfortable
- Share trip details with friend/family
- Use in-app emergency button if needed

**❌ DON'T:**
- Drive under influence of alcohol or drugs
- Use phone while driving (except mounted GPS)
- Exceed speed limits or drive recklessly
- Distract driver while vehicle is moving
- Enter vehicle if you feel unsafe

### Personal Safety

**✅ DO:**
- Meet driver/rider at well-lit, public locations when possible
- Confirm vehicle details match app (make, model, plate number)
- Sit in back seat as rider (personal preference)
- Keep valuables secure and out of sight
- End ride immediately if you feel threatened

**❌ DON'T:**
- Share personal information (home address, work, etc.)
- Accept/provide rides to/from unauthorized locations
- Engage in physical contact beyond handshake
- Travel with someone without booking them in app

## 2. Respect and Courtesy

### General Conduct

**✅ DO:**
- Greet politely and professionally
- Use respectful language
- Be punctual and ready at pickup time
- Communicate clearly about destinations/stops
- Thank driver/rider at end of trip
- Leave honest, constructive ratings

**❌ DON'T:**
- Use offensive, discriminatory, or abusive language
- Make unwanted advances or flirtatious comments
- Raise voice or threaten others
- Slam doors or damage property
- Eat/drink without permission
- Smoke or vape in vehicle

### Inclusive Environment

**ZERO TOLERANCE for discrimination based on:**
- Race or ethnicity
- Religion or beliefs
- Gender or gender identity
- Sexual orientation
- Age
- Disability
- National origin
- Tribe or region
- Language

**✅ DO:**
- Accept all riders regardless of destination
- Accommodate riders with disabilities
- Respect cultural and religious practices
- Use preferred names and pronouns
- Provide service equally to all

**❌ DON'T:**
- Refuse service based on protected characteristics
- Make assumptions about people
- Ask invasive personal questions
- Mock or ridicule others
- Refuse reasonable accommodations

---

## 3. Professional Standards

### For Drivers

**Vehicle Standards:**
- ✅ Clean interior and exterior
- ✅ Functioning air conditioning/windows
- ✅ No strong odors (smoke, air freshener, etc.)
- ✅ Working seatbelts for all passengers
- ✅ No visible damage or mechanical issues

**Personal Presentation:**
- ✅ Clean, appropriate clothing
- ✅ Good personal hygiene
- ✅ Professional demeanor
- ❌ No revealing or offensive clothing
- ❌ No strong cologne/perfume

**Service Quality:**
- ✅ Confirm destination before starting
- ✅ Follow GPS navigation (or rider's preference)
- ✅ Drive smoothly and safely
- ✅ Offer charging cable if available
- ✅ Help with luggage when appropriate
- ❌ Don't make unnecessary conversation if rider prefers quiet
- ❌ Don't play music too loudly
- ❌ Don't make extra stops without consent

### For Riders

**Preparedness:**
- ✅ Be ready within 5 minutes of driver arrival
- ✅ Provide clear pickup instructions
- ✅ Have correct payment ready
- ✅ Bring reasonable amount of luggage
- ❌ Don't make driver wait excessively
- ❌ Don't request multiple stops without warning

**Vehicle Respect:**
- ✅ Close doors gently
- ✅ Keep vehicle clean
- ✅ Respect driver's property
- ✅ Ask permission before opening windows
- ❌ Don't put feet on seats
- ❌ Don't leave trash
- ❌ Don't adjust controls without asking

---

## 4. Honest and Ethical Behavior

### Fraud Prevention

**❌ NEVER:**
- Create fake accounts
- Book rides with no intention to pay
- Cancel repeatedly to avoid payment
- Manipulate ratings system
- Coordinate off-platform payments to avoid fees
- Share account credentials
- Impersonate others
- Submit false reports

**✅ ALWAYS:**
- Pay agreed fare
- Report legitimate issues only
- Rate honestly and fairly
- Use your own account
- Follow platform payment processes

### Accountability

**If something goes wrong:**
- ✅ Report immediately through proper channels
- ✅ Provide accurate description of incident
- ✅ Take responsibility for your actions
- ✅ Cooperate with investigations
- ❌ Don't retaliate or harass
- ❌ Don't make false accusations
- ❌ Don't attempt to resolve disputes outside platform

---

## 5. Communication Standards

### In-App Communication

**✅ DO:**
- Keep messages brief and relevant
- Communicate about ride logistics only
- Be polite and professional
- Respond promptly to driver/rider messages
- Use clear language

**❌ DON'T:**
- Send spam or promotional content
- Share external links or phone numbers
- Use profanity or offensive language
- Send unwanted messages after ride
- Request personal social media

### Phone Calls

**When calling is appropriate:**
- Cannot find pickup location
- Running late
- Need to change destination
- Vehicle breakdown or emergency
- Clarifying ride details

**Keep calls:**
- Short and to the point
- Professional in tone
- Related to current ride only
- During reasonable hours

---

## 6. Ratings and Reviews

### Rating Fairly

**Consider rating down for:**
- Safety violations
- Unprofessional behavior
- Vehicle condition issues
- Route problems
- Disrespectful conduct

**DO NOT rate down for:**
- Traffic delays beyond driver's control
- Weather conditions
- Personal biases or prejudices
- Requesting reasonable fare
- Not engaging in conversation (if service was otherwise good)

### Writing Reviews

**✅ DO:**
- Be specific and constructive
- Focus on facts and behavior
- Keep it brief and relevant
- Mention both positives and negatives
- Help others make informed decisions

**❌ DON'T:**
- Include personal information
- Use offensive language
- Make threats or accusations
- Write fake reviews
- Retaliate for low rating received

## 7. Prohibited Items and Activities

### ABSOLUTELY NO:

**Weapons:**
- Firearms
- Knives or blades
- Pepper spray
- Any weapon prohibited by law

**Illegal Substances:**
- Drugs or narcotics
- Stolen goods
- Contraband

**Dangerous Materials:**
- Explosives or fireworks
- Flammable liquids
- Toxic chemicals
- Open containers of alcohol

**Inappropriate Behavior:**
- Sexual activity or solicitation
- Verbal or physical harassment
- Threatening behavior
- Damage to property
- Recording others without consent

### Permitted (with Consent):

**Pets:**
- Small pets in carriers: Usually okay
- Service animals: Always allowed
- Large pets: Ask driver first

**Children:**
- Infants: Bring appropriate car seat
- Young children: Adult supervision required
- Unaccompanied minors: Not allowed (must be 18+)

**Luggage:**
- Reasonable amount for vehicle size
- Ask about large items before booking
- Driver may refuse oversized items

## 8. Special Considerations

### Accessibility

**Supporting riders with disabilities:**
- ✅ Drivers must accommodate service animals
- ✅ Assist with wheelchair folding if safe
- ✅ Allow extra time for boarding
- ✅ Be patient and understanding
- ❌ Never refuse ride due to disability

**Supporting drivers with disabilities:**
- ✅ Respect driver's accessibility needs
- ✅ Follow their boarding instructions
- ✅ Be patient with communication differences

### Cultural Sensitivity

**Tanzania is diverse:**
- Respect religious practices (prayer times, dietary restrictions)
- Accommodate traditional dress
- Be sensitive to language barriers
- Respect tribal and regional differences
- Avoid religious or political debates

## 9. Reporting Violations

### When to Report

**Report immediately for:**
- Safety threats or dangerous driving
- Harassment or discrimination
- Fraud or scam attempts
- Impaired driving
- Unauthorized charges
- Physical altercations
- Inappropriate conduct

### How to Report

**In-App:**
1. Go to ride history
2. Select problematic ride
3. Tap "Report Issue"
4. Choose category
5. Describe incident in detail
6. Submit evidence (photos, screenshots)

**Email:** safety@raut.co.tz

**Emergency:** Use in-app emergency button or call local police

### What Happens Next

1. **Investigation:** We review all reports within 24 hours
2. **Action:** May result in warning, suspension, or permanent ban
3. **Response:** You'll receive update on outcome
4. **Appeal:** Users can appeal decisions at appeals@raut.co.tz

**We take all reports seriously.** False reports are also violations and may result in account suspension.

---

## 10. Consequences for Violations

### Warning System

**First Violation (Minor):**
- Written warning
- Required to acknowledge guidelines
- Probationary period

**Second Violation:**
- 7-day suspension
- Mandatory safety education
- Final warning

**Third Violation or Serious First Offense:**
- Permanent account deactivation
- No refunds for services
- May be reported to authorities

### Serious Violations (Immediate Permanent Ban):
- Violence or threats of violence
- Sexual harassment or assault
- Driving under influence
- Criminal activity
- Discrimination
- Fraud or theft
- Weapons possession
- Repeated safety violations

### Appeals Process

If you believe action was taken in error:
1. Email appeals@raut.co.tz within 14 days
2. Provide explanation and evidence
3. Decision reviewed by different team member
4. Final decision communicated within 7 days
5. Appeal decisions are final
