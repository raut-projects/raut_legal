**Ride-Sharing Services:**

**Transit Information Services:**
- Real-time bus location tracking
- Route planning for DART BRT and daladalas
- Bus stop information

## 4. Rider Terms

### 4.1 Requesting Rides

**Booking:**
- Enter pickup location (current location or manual selection)
- Enter destination
- Choose ride type (Private or DUO)
- Choose vehicle type (Car, Bajaji, Motorcycle)
- Review fare estimate
- Confirm booking

**DUO Rides:**
- By requesting DUO, you agree to share the vehicle with other riders
- Your dropoff location is visible to driver immediately
- Detours may occur to accommodate other riders
- Fare is reduced compared to private rides
- Maximum 2-3 riders per vehicle depending on capacity
- You may be asked to change seats for optimal routing

### 4.2 Driver Matching

- Driver assignment based on proximity, availability, and route
- You can see driver's name, photo, vehicle, rating, and ETA
- Driver may decline your request
- If no driver accepts within reasonable time, request expires

### 4.3 Pickup

- Be ready at pickup location within 5 minutes of driver arrival
- Driver may wait up to 5 minutes before canceling
- Contact driver if you need more time
- Canceling after driver arrives may incur cancellation fee

### 4.4 During the Ride

**Safety:**
- Wear seatbelt at all times
- Do not distract driver while driving
- Follow driver's reasonable instructions
- Report any safety concerns immediately via in-app emergency button

**Conduct:**
- Be respectful and courteous to driver
- Do not eat or drink without driver permission
- Do not smoke or vape in vehicle
- Do not bring illegal items
- Clean up any mess you make
- Comply with vehicle capacity limits

**Modifications:**
- Route changes must be agreed by driver
- Additional stops may incur extra charges
- Significant destination changes require new booking

### 4.5 Payment

**Fare Calculation:**
- Based on distance, estimated time, and ride type
- Shown as estimate before booking
- Final fare may differ due to actual route, traffic, or tolls
- DUO fares are discounted based on distance shared

**Payment Methods:**
- Cash payment to driver
- Mobile money (M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money) - future feature
- You must have sufficient payment means before booking

**Payment Process:**
- Cash: Pay driver directly at end of ride
- Mobile money: Processed through app
- Receipt provided in ride history

**Pricing:**
- Base fare + per-kilometer rate + per-minute rate
- Pricing varies by vehicle type and ride type
- Surge pricing may apply during high demand (future feature)
- Tolls and parking fees are additional

### 4.6 Cancellations

**Before Pickup:**
- Free cancellation within 2 minutes of booking
- After 2 minutes: Cancellation fee may apply
- After driver arrives: Cancellation fee charged

**Driver Cancellation:**
- If driver cancels, no fee charged to you
- You may report driver for unreasonable cancellation

### 4.7 Ratings and Reviews

After each ride, you can:
- Rate driver from 1-5 stars
- Leave optional text review
- Ratings are anonymous to driver
- Consistently low ratings may result in driver suspension
- Be honest and fair in your ratings

### 4.8 Lost Items

If you leave items in vehicle:
- Contact driver through app immediately
- We are not responsible for lost items
- Driver is not obligated to return items but we encourage it
- Coordinate retrieval with driver directly

### 5.2 Driver Requirements

**Minimum Qualifications:**
- At least 21 years old
- Valid Tanzanian driver's license for appropriate vehicle class
- At least 2 years driving experience
- Clean driving record (no major violations)
- Vehicle meets safety and condition standards
- Valid vehicle insurance
- Vehicle registration documents

**Vehicles:**
- Maximum 10 years old for cars
- Maximum 5 years old for bajajis
- Pass safety inspection
- Clean and well-maintained
- Functioning seatbelts
- No cosmetic damage that affects appearance

**Background:**
- No criminal record for violent crimes
- No record of reckless driving
- Right to work in Tanzania

### 5.3 Driver Verification

Before driving, you must:
- Submit driver's license photo (front and back)
- Submit vehicle registration documents
- Submit vehicle insurance certificate
- Submit clear photos of vehicle (all angles)
- Submit National ID or TIN
- Pass background check
- Complete in-app safety training

Documents reviewed within 48 hours. Account approved or rejected with reason.

### 5.4 Accepting Rides

**Your Choice:**
- You may accept or decline any ride request
- No penalty for declining
- Accepting ride creates contract with rider
- You agree to provide transportation as requested

**Response Time:**
- Respond to requests within 30 seconds
- Excessive non-responses may affect your priority in matching algorithm

**Cancellation:**
- Cancel only for legitimate reasons (emergency, rider unreachable, safety concern)
- Excessive cancellations may result in suspension
- If rider is not at pickup within 5 minutes, you may cancel without penalty

### 5.5 Providing Rides

**Safety First:**
- Obey all traffic laws
- Do not exceed speed limits
- Use turn signals
- Maintain safe following distance
- Do not use phone while driving (except mounted GPS navigation)
- Ensure all passengers wear seatbelts
- No driving under influence of alcohol or drugs

**Conduct:**
- Be professional and courteous
- Greet rider by name
- Confirm destination before starting
- Keep vehicle clean
- No smoking in vehicle
- No inappropriate conversations
- Respect rider's preference for conversation/silence
- Do not ask for rider's personal contact information

**Route:**
- Follow GPS navigation provided by app
- Take most efficient route
- Rider may request specific route
- Notify rider of significant delays
- Do not make unauthorized stops

**DUO Rides:**
- Multiple riders may be added to your ride
- Each pickup/dropoff shown in app
- Optimize route for all riders
- Be fair to all passengers
- Communicate clearly about stops

### 5.6 Earnings and Payments

**Fare Structure:**
- You receive percentage of total fare
- RAUT keeps platform fee (typically 20-25%)
- You keep 100% of tips (when tipping feature launched)
- Fare breakdown shown in app

**Payout:**

**Expenses:**
- You pay for fuel
