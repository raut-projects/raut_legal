## 1. Introduction

Welcome to RAUT ("we," "our," or "us"). RAUT Technologies operates a technology platform that connects riders with drivers for on-demand transportation services and provides real-time public transit information across Tanzania.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal information when you use:
- RAUT Rider mobile application
- RAUT Driver mobile application
- RAUT website and related services

By using our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

**Contact Information:**
- **Company:** RAUT Technologies
- **Location:** Dar es Salaam, Tanzania
- **Email:** privacy@raut.co.tz
- **Support:** support@raut.co.tz

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

**Account Registration:**

- Full name
- Profile photograph (optional)
- Email address (optional)

**Driver-Specific Information:**

**Device Information:**
- Device model and manufacturer
- Operating system and version
- Mobile network information
- IP address
- App version
- Unique device identifiers
- Advertising ID (for analytics)

**Usage Data:**
- Search queries and history
- Recent searches (cached locally and on server)

- App interaction patterns
- Features used
- Time spent in app
- Crash reports and error logs

**Ride Data:**
- Ride history (date, time, route, fare)
- Driver assigned to ride
- Vehicle details
- Ride status updates
- Payment method used
- Ratings and reviews
- Estimated vs actual fare
- Route polylines (GPS coordinates of path taken)

**Transit Data:**
- Bus routes searched
- Stops selected
- Transit trip planning queries
- BLE beacon scan data (for crowdsourced bus tracking)

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties

**Mapping Services:**

**SMS Verification:**

**Payment Processors:**
- Payment confirmation from mobile money providers

**Social Media (if you choose to link):**

## 5. Data Retention

### 5.1 Active Accounts

While your account is active, we retain:
- Profile information: Indefinitely
- Ride history: 7 years (for tax and legal compliance)
- Location data: 90 days of detailed GPS logs
- Payment records: 7 years
- Support messages: 3 years

### 5.2 Deleted Accounts

When you delete your account:
- Profile information: Deleted within 30 days
- Ride history: Anonymized (driver/rider names removed) and retained for 7 years for legal/tax purposes
- Location data: Deleted within 30 days
- Ratings: Anonymized and retained (cannot be deleted as they affect other users' ratings)
- Payment records: Retained for 7 years in anonymized form for accounting

### 5.3 Legal Holds

Data subject to legal holds, ongoing investigations, or disputes will be retained until matter is resolved.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

### 6.1 Access Your Data

You have the right to access your personal information:
- View in-app: Profile, ride history, payment history
- Request full data export: Email privacy@raut.co.tz

### 6.2 Update Your Data

You can update information directly in the app:
- Edit profile (name, photo)
- Update phone number (requires re-verification)
- Modify payment methods

### 6.3 Delete Your Data

You can request account deletion:
- In-app: Settings → Account → Delete Account
- Email: privacy@raut.co.tz with subject "Account Deletion"
- Deletion processed within 30 days
- Some data retained in anonymized form for legal compliance

### 6.4 Location Permissions

**Riders:**
- **"While Using App":** Required for ride booking
- **Background Location:** Optional, improves pickup accuracy
- Manage in: Phone Settings → RAUT → Location

**Drivers:**
- **"Always" / Background Location:** Required when online to show live position
- Without this, you cannot accept rides
- Manage in: Phone Settings → RAUT → Location

### 6.5 Opt-Out of Marketing

- Push notifications: Settings → Notifications → Disable Promotional
- SMS marketing: Reply STOP to any promotional SMS
- Email marketing: Click unsubscribe in any marketing email

### 6.6 Data Portability

Request a copy of your data in machine-readable format:
- Email privacy@raut.co.tz
- Receive JSON/CSV export within 30 days

### 6.7 Object to Processing

You may object to certain data processing:
- Marketing and promotional uses
- Non-essential analytics
- Note: Cannot object to processing required for service delivery

## 7. Data Security

### 7.1 Technical Measures

- **Encryption:** All data transmitted over HTTPS/TLS 1.3
- **Database:** PostgreSQL with encrypted connections and at-rest encryption
- **Authentication:** Phone-based OTP (no passwords stored)
- **Access Control:** Role-based permissions, principle of least privilege
- **WebSocket Security:** Authenticated connections with short-lived tokens
- **API Security:** Rate limiting, request validation, SQL injection prevention

### 7.2 Physical Security

- Servers hosted in secure data centers
- 24/7 monitoring and intrusion detection
- Regular security audits

### 7.3 Organizational Measures

- Employee access restricted to authorized personnel only
- Background checks for employees with data access
- Regular security training
- Incident response procedures
- Data breach notification protocols

### 7.4 Third-Party Security

All service providers must:
- Maintain industry-standard security practices
- Sign data protection agreements
- Undergo security assessments
- Notify us of any breaches

### 7.5 Your Responsibility

- Keep your phone secure with PIN/biometric lock
- Do not share OTP codes with anyone
- Log out on shared devices
- Report lost/stolen phones immediately
- Review ride receipts for unauthorized charges
